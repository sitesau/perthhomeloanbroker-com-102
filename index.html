Questions for Your First Call

Checks to use on the first call include confirming which loan type the broker handles most often, especially if the file involves first home buyer support, investment borrowing, construction progress payments or commercial finance. Ask whether the comparison is likely to involve a major bank, a specialist lender, or both.

Ask what documents are needed before the discussion becomes a real comparison rather than a general chat. Common documents include proof of income, identification, and details of the property you intend to purchase or refinance. Ask which feature is driving lender choice, such as equity access, an interest only period, a refinance restructure or a construction draw schedule.
